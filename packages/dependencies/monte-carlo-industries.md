## **Executive summary**

Monte Carlo’s vertical strategy should split into two motions:

1. **Current-core data observability verticals**: financial services/fintech, SaaS/technology, retail/e-commerce/CPG, media/advertising/entertainment, healthcare/life sciences. These are already highly data-dependent industries where broken data directly affects reporting, revenue, customer experience, compliance, or machine-learning systems. Monte Carlo’s own historical positioning named fintech, e-commerce, media, B2B software, and retail as leading verticals, with customers such as Fox, Vimeo, ThredUp, and PagerDuty cited in earlier company material.  
2. **Near- and mid-term AI-agent growth verticals**: financial services, healthcare/life sciences, insurance, telecom, manufacturing/supply chain, Salesforce-heavy enterprise GTM/customer operations, and regulated B2B software. These verticals are where “data observability” expands into **AI trust, agent reliability, governed automation, operational risk reduction, and customer-facing AI assurance**.

The core vertical pattern is this:

| Vertical | Monte Carlo’s strongest value story |
| ----- | ----- |
| Financial services / fintech | Prevent bad data from corrupting risk, compliance, fraud, customer, and financial decisioning. |
| Insurance | Improve trust in underwriting, claims, risk models, regulatory reporting, and AI-assisted servicing. |
| Healthcare providers | Protect patient experience, compliance, care operations, revenue cycle, and AI-assisted workflows. |
| Life sciences / pharma | Avoid research, trial, manufacturing, supply, and regulatory-data errors. |
| Retail / e-commerce / marketplaces | Protect revenue, inventory, pricing, personalization, attribution, and customer journeys. |
| CPG / manufacturing / supply chain | Prevent operational decisions from being made on bad planning, inventory, plant, sustainability, and trade-promotion data. |
| SaaS / B2B technology | Improve product analytics, customer health, usage metrics, self-service analytics, and AI product reliability. |
| Media / advertising / entertainment | Protect ad delivery, third-party data streams, recommendations, audience analytics, and revenue operations. |
| Telecom | Improve network, billing, churn, field operations, customer experience, and AI support-agent reliability. |
| Public sector / education / regulated services | Support auditability, reporting accuracy, citizen/student outcomes, and responsible AI controls. |

The sales nuance: in some industries Monte Carlo is sold as **risk control**; in others as **revenue protection**; in others as **operational efficiency**; and increasingly, in many, as **production AI trust infrastructure**.

# **1\. Financial services and fintech**

## **Why this is likely a top Monte Carlo vertical**

Financial services is one of the most natural markets because the cost of bad data is unusually high. Monte Carlo’s financial-services page explicitly frames the value proposition as making data quality as stringent as industry regulations, with use cases around third-party data integrity, proactive incident resolution, risk management, and compliance.
