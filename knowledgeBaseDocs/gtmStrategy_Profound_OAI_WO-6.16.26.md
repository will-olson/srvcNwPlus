**SECTION 1**

# **Executive summary**

**Profound should position itself as the AI Search Intelligence and Activation Platform: the system that reveals real conversational demand, measures how brands are represented across consumer answer engines, diagnoses the sources and crawler behavior behind that representation, and turns the findings into governed action.**

The category is rapidly fragmenting into four archetypes: low-cost prompt trackers, traditional SEO suites adding AI modules, content-production platforms adding AEO workflows, and enterprise experience suites adding LLM optimization. Prompt tracking alone is therefore becoming a feature rather than a durable category. Profound’s defensible position is the integrated data and operating loop: demand intelligence \-\> answer-engine measurement \-\> citation and narrative diagnosis \-\> crawler/referral evidence \-\> agent-driven execution.

| Recommended category languageLead with “AI Search Intelligence and Activation” for executive audiences. Use “Answer Engine Optimization platform” when the buyer already uses AEO terminology. Avoid presenting Profound as merely an “AI rank tracker,” which compresses the product into the most commoditizing part of the market. |
| :---- |

## **Top strategic recommendations**

• **Sell control, not curiosity.** Frame AI discovery as a material brand, demand, and revenue surface that is currently unowned and unmeasured.

• **Differentiate on methodology before feature count.** Real prompt demand, direct consumer-interface capture, daily runs, and multi-engine coverage are more defensible than generic dashboard breadth.

• **Make the closed loop the centerpiece.** Connect Prompt Volumes, Answer Engine Insights, Agent Analytics, and Agents in every enterprise story.

• **Verticalize around the decision journey.** B2B software, financial services, retail/ecommerce, travel, and healthcare have distinct query patterns, proof requirements, and risk profiles.

• **De-risk the purchase with a measurement-first proof.** Baseline a defined topic, competitor set, geography, and buyer journey; then test a small set of interventions against visibility, citation, crawler, referral, and pipeline indicators.

• **Separate platform value from traffic volume.** AI answers influence consideration even when users do not click. Measure recommendation presence, narrative accuracy, citation ownership, high-intent referrals, and downstream opportunity creation.

## **Positioning statement**

| Core positioningFor brands whose customers increasingly research and compare products in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI experiences, and other answer engines, Profound is the AI Search Intelligence and Activation Platform that shows what audiences are asking, how AI represents the brand, which sources and crawlers shape those answers, and what to do next. Unlike lightweight trackers, legacy SEO add-ons, or content generators, Profound connects real conversational demand, consumer-interface measurement, web-level evidence, and agentic execution in one operating system. |

## **2.2 Competitive dynamics**

• **Prompt tracking is commoditizing.** Competitors increasingly offer mentions, citations, share of voice, and prompt histories. Profound should make methodology, representativeness, and workflow integration the comparison criteria.

• **Incumbents will bundle.** SEO, CRM, and experience suites can discount AI visibility as an add-on. Profound must win on depth, pace, specialization, and cross-functional use.

• **Execution is becoming expected.** Buyers do not want another dashboard. The Profound Agents narrative should appear early enough to prevent AirOps, Jasper, or Adobe from owning the “insight to action” frame.

• **Attribution remains the category’s credibility test.** Referral volume is only one outcome. Profound should standardize a broader measurement model spanning visibility, narrative, citations, crawler access, referrals, conversions, and influenced pipeline.

• **Methodology is a battleground.** Direct browser capture versus API-first collection, custom prompts versus modeled prompt universes, and single runs versus repeated daily sampling create materially different outputs.

# **Mid-funnel objection handling**

## **6.1 Response framework: CARED**

| STEP | SELLER ACTION | EXAMPLE |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Clarify | Identify the exact concern, stakeholder, evidence threshold, and decision impact. | “When you say the data is noisy, are you concerned about prompt selection, repeatability, or differences from the consumer interface?” |
| Acknowledge | Validate the legitimate part of the objection without conceding the conclusion. | “You are right that generated answers are probabilistic; a single manual check should not drive a budget decision.” |
| Reframe | Move from the prospect’s narrow frame to the business risk or evaluation criterion. | “The question is not whether every answer is identical. It is whether we can measure the distribution consistently enough to detect competitive and narrative patterns.” |
| Evidence | Use methodology, product proof, customer proof, or a scoped diagnostic. | Show daily runs, raw answers, browser capture, citations, and a topic-level trend rather than a top-line score only. |
| Decision advance | Agree on a test, owner, asset, or next meeting that resolves the concern. | “Let’s include your analytics lead and validate three prompts across Profound and your manual method before finalizing the pilot.” |
